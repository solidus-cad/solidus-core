- Name a fork, product or service "Solidus" or anything likely to cause confusion with it.

- Distribute **official builds**: signed installers, the online service, the library server and licenses are reserved to the project. A build from source must remove the name and logo and must not connect to the official server without permission.

## Forks
A fork must replace the name and the logos (`apps/web/public/*.svg`, icons in `apps/desktop/src-tauri/icons`), and must not connect to the official Solidus services without permission.
