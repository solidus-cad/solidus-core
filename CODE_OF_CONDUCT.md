# Code of Conduct

This Code of Conduct is based on the *Contributor Covenant*, version 2.1 (https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior
- Show empathy and kindness;
- respect differing viewpoints and experiences;
- give and accept constructive feedback gracefully;
- take ownership of mistakes and learn from them;
- prioritize what is best for the community.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention;
- trolling, insults, personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing others' private information (address, email, etc.) without permission;
- any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement
The project maintainers clarify and enforce these rules. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not comply, and may temporarily or permanently ban any contributor whose behavior they consider inappropriate.

## Scope
This Code applies in all project spaces (repositories, issues, discussions, events) and when a person officially represents the project in public.

## Reporting
Report unacceptable behavior to: **contact@solidus-cad.com**. All reports are reviewed confidentially and fairly.
